About

We take a production from the screenplay to the last receipt: reading the script for spaces and props, planning the look with the director, sourcing and making what the scenes need, dressing the set, and closing the art budget after the shoot.

The team is two production designers and one assistant, all trained in design. Most of our work so far is short film, alongside broadcast, and we are moving into music video and drama.

Tools we build

A small art department loses most of its time to paperwork. We build our own operating tools with Claude, by Anthropic, and run them in Google Sheets and Drive.

  • Production sheetOne sheet per production that links the scene list, the prop list, the estimate and the settlement, so a change in one place carries through to the rest.In use. Our latest short tracked 85 props in it.
  • Expense settlementExpenses and receipts go in, a settlement document ready to submit comes out.In use.
  • Job intakeReading incoming job alerts, checking them against our rates and calendar, and drafting a reply for a person to approve.In progress.
  • Screenplay breakdownFrom a script to a first scene and prop breakdown with a draft budget, ready before the first meeting.Next.
